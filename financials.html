How It's Calculated

Two layers. First, every team gets a Base Score (season-level, recomputed each GW) blending: Standings (HIGH weight -- 45%), Top 11 ownership (MEDIUM -- 25%), Trophy count (LOW -- 15%), MBP (VERY LOW -- 5%), and Momentum/streak (our own addition, 10%, 0 until real win/loss results exist). That score becomes each team's share of a total league fan pool (stadium capacity × 0.35, a static anchor -- see fans_algo.py for the full reasoning on why the total stays fixed while shares move).
Second, for a specific matchup: home + away fan interest combine, then get boosted for a big occasion -- Derby Day (our pick, HIGH, +35%), #1 vs #2 clash (VERY LOW, +8%), bottom-of-the-table drama (VERY LOW, +8%), and upset buzz (our own addition, +12%, 0 until real results exist) -- then get capped at the home stadium's capacity. Overflow (interest beyond capacity) is simply lost demand for that game, not redistributed to other games -- the simple choice; we considered spillover but it adds real complexity for a benefit we can't calibrate yet.
Other ideas considered but not built yet: new-signing hype (a fan bump the week after a notable transfer -- we only have one transfer on record so far, not enough to calibrate), stadium-expansion opening-week bump, and tying fan interest to how a team's real-life EPL players are performing that week (deferred: needs another live data source and is fragile to build well right now).
