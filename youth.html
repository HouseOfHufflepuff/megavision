Rulez 4.1(6) · Youth Draft History is the list · Drafted '27 is not youth

What counts

A Youth Player is a name on that team's Youth Draft History. Source is the Youth sheet plus youth_draft_picks in mega.db. Promoted status follows a Youth Player or Youth Legend contract on the books.

A Drafted '27 tag is a senior auction pick. Age 23 or under does not make that player a Youth Player. Do not mail owners about drafted U23s for promote-or-drop.

Four free Mega Sr league games. A fifth start is promote ($4 / $4 / $4.40) or drop (full remaining salary is dead money). Promoted at 23 or under does not count against the 9. Cup and Euro games do not count. Full history is on each team page. Rule text is Rulez 4.1(6).
