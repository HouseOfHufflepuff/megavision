Notes

  • The League Pot is a running ledger, not a per-week reset — stadium fees, the transfer levy, and IRP fees land once, when they happen; salaries and tickets are the only lines that move every gameweek.
  • Pot Balance is floored at $0 for the Mega Fund calc below: TV Bonus is a payout, never a charge back to teams, so a negative running total just means the league needs more inflow before it pays out, not a bill sent to anyone.
  • Mega Fund & TV Bonus is a live "if the season ended today" preview, not a final payout — it recomputes every rebuild from the current pot balance and current standings, and will keep shifting as more weeks are played. Postseason (Champions League/Europa) isn't shown here yet since there are no results to pay out.
  • Since TV Bonus + Postseason together redistribute 100% of the Mega Fund, and the TV Bonus split is weighted toward the top of the standings (40% to 1st, down to 0% for 12th), most individual teams will net-lose relative to what they paid into the pot across a season — that's the intended reward mechanism for finishing well, not money leaving the league. Nothing is destroyed: every dollar collected eventually pays back out to somebody.

How It's Calculated

Two layers. First, every team's real fan count is a literal sum, not a weighted score: League Record standing (200 fans for 1st, down to 20 for 12th, by real win/loss/draw record), Overall Scoring standing (100 fans for 1st, down to 35 for 8th by cumulative season points -- a different ranking than League Record; 9th-12th get 0), Top XI (30 fans each for the single top-scoring F/M/D that week, 10 fans each for the other 8 Best-11 slots), and Legacy (title history -- 10 fans per League/CL title in the last 3 years, 5 per Europa/FA Cup/Citadel Cup, 1 per Super Cup/Community Shield). That sum is the team's actual fan count -- not a share of a pool, the real number.
Second, for a specific matchup: home + away real fan counts combine, then get boosted for a big occasion -- Derby Day (+35%), #1 vs #2 clash (+8%), bottom-of-the-table drama (+8%), and upset buzz (+12%, 0 until real results exist) -- then get capped at the home stadium's capacity. Overflow (interest beyond capacity) is simply lost demand for that game, not redistributed to other games. This matchup-day bonus layer is our own flavor addition, not in the documented rules -- open question to the commissioner: keep it, or strip attendance down to the literal ruleset with no game-day multipliers?
Known simplification: Legacy title fans only cover the current season (26/27) -- no multi-season title history is tracked yet, so the "last 3 years" vs "all-time" distinction in the rule can't produce a different number yet.
